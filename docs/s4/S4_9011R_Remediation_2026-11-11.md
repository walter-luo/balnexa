## 2. Decisions taken under the delegation — `DELEGATED_DECISION`

| ID | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| D-9011R-1 | No hex/UUID exemption in text. In JSON, only a whole canonical UUID or digest under an id/hash/key/nonce/token key, never under a labelled key. Multi-group windows inside a UUID-shaped substring are not sequences | Closes the padding bypass (R-01) while random UUIDs and hashes in payloads stay usable. Measured: a random UUID in free text is flagged in 0.2 % of cases, a SHA-256 in 1.5 %; test clients now send canonical UUIDs as idempotency keys (the Worker already does) |
| D-9011R-2 | Separators: spaces and dashes in any grouping (at most three groups of at least two digits, any grouping near a label); dot, slash, underscore, middle dot, bullet, bare comma, tab and line break only in the printed 3-3-3 form | Measured on the benign corpus: decimal amounts (`10140.0000`), comma lists (`[403, 409, 422]`), dates and TSV columns would otherwise become candidates. FP-SIN-01: overall 0.83 % (v1.0: 0.79 %); all categories except random nine-digit references 0.06 % |
| D-9011R-3 | The repository scan (guard C6 only) reports a hit inside a whole UUID or hex string of 32+ characters as a machine token and does not fail on it; reviewed history entries are listed by blob SHA in `tools/s4/sin_repo_scan_allow.txt` (five versions of one document line with an object-id list) | Commit hashes, manifests and dump rows are in the repository; history is public and is not rewritten. | D-9011R-4 | PDF pages are rendered with PDFium and OCRed, judged with the native rules (not R_OCR9) | A cross-check of what the page shows; R_OCR9 on every rendered statement would quarantine account and reference numbers. Image documents keep the OCR rules |
